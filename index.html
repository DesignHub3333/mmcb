McB Valorant Bot Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the McB Valorant Discord Bot ("Bot", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Bot or interact with our Discord community.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the Bot, we may collect limited information necessary for the Bot to operate.

  • Discord User ID
  • Discord username and display name
  • Discord server/guild ID
  • Discord channel and role information required for Bot functions
  • Information submitted through applications, tickets, or forms
  • Valorant/Riot ID information if voluntarily provided
  • Information required to provide premium membership or community services

2. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Provide and operate Bot features
  • Process applications and support requests
  • Manage community roles and permissions
  • Provide premium membership features
  • Organize tournaments and community events
  • Prevent abuse, spam, fraud, and misuse of the Bot
  • Maintain and improve the Bot
  • Respond to support requests

3. Discord Information

The Bot operates through Discord. When you interact with the Bot, Discord may provide information such as your Discord User ID, username, server information, and other information permitted by Discord's API and the permissions granted to the Bot.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Valorant Information

If you voluntarily provide your Riot ID, Valorant rank, region, or other gaming information, this information may be used for community applications, matchmaking, tournaments, rankings, or related community features.

5. Data Storage

Information may be stored on servers or databases used to operate the Bot. We take reasonable measures to protect stored information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Information may only be shared when reasonably necessary to:

  • Operate and maintain the Bot
  • Provide requested services
  • Protect the security of the community
  • Comply with applicable laws or legal requests

7. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the Bot, provide requested services, maintain security, resolve disputes, or comply with legal obligations.

8. Data Deletion

You may request deletion of information associated with your use of the Bot, where applicable.

To request data deletion, contact the McB Valorant community administrators through the official Discord server.

9. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws. If you believe that we have collected information from a child improperly, please contact the community administrators so that the information can be reviewed and deleted where appropriate.

10. Third-Party Services

The Bot may interact with third-party services such as Discord and, where applicable, Riot Games services or APIs. These services have their own privacy policies and terms. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request information or deletion, please contact the McB Valorant community administrators through the official Discord server.
